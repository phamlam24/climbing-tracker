**Experience:** Visited once on morning of June 2nd, 2026. 

## Short Review (no long reviews since I've only been here once):
 - Route Setting: Amazing quality, focus on static climbing, grip strength and footwork, a lot of different problem types, a lot of horizontal movement.
 - Gym does not use traditional grading system, but uses a scale from 1 (VB/V0) to 11 (hardest climbs). Personally I just do `number - 1 = V-grade`
 - Facilities: ~8-9 wall sections with ~50-60 climbs, one section reset per week, moonboard, kilterboard, hangboard, rental shoe and chalk available.
 - Pretty hot in the summer, but manageable.
 - Crowd: Not really, but I don't know for sure since I went at non peak hours. Very friendly people.
