**Experience:** 2 Weeks Climbing - currently on membership

## Logistics:
 - Grading System: Color Grading System:
   - Red: V0
   - Orange: V1
   - Yellow: V1-V2 (mostly V2)
   - Green: V2-V3 (mostly V3)
   - Blue: V3-V5 (mostly V4-V5)
   - Pink+ no idea since way out of my grade
 - Locations: Two locations
   - Tay Ho location first location, two floors, you can top off, but overall pretty small, ~40 problem, all terrains
   - My Dinh location newer one, ~50 boulders, mostly flat and smaller overhangs
 - Pretty slow reset - one section get resetted every week in one of the locations, so expect ~4 months for a cycle, project however hard you want
 - Not all volumes can be used for every climb - this makes some unique challenges moving "around" the volumes
 - Fully AC-ed, honestly a big plus for Hanoi summer

## Boulder Setting:
 - Focus: Footwork (expect a lot of flags/foot swaps/scary committal moves), cross moves, learning/skill training boulders
 - Less of: Comp-style, very difficult boulders, bad handholds (crimps/pockets), cave
 - Very fun setting usually, you will do a lot of cool moves

## Others:
 - Very good and friendly staff, ask them to help with beta if needed
